01

Corporate Identity & Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy (“Privacy Policy” or “Policy”) governs how DeccanIDentity Software Pvt. Ltd. (“DeccanIDentity”, “Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”), an enterprise technology corporation headquartered in Hyderabad, Telangana, India, collects, uses, processes, stores, and safeguards personal information obtained through our official website (deccanidentity.com), demonstration platforms (demo.deccanidentity.in, apps.deccanidentity.in), enterprise service portals, communications, and recruitment/internship channels.

By accessing our website, inquiring about our Zero-Trust Identity and Access Management (IAM) systems, enrolling in technical programs, or utilizing our software engineering services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

02

Information We Collect

In delivering our specialized cybersecurity, cloud engineering, and enterprise software solutions, we collect information directly from you, automatically through your interaction with our digital platforms, and via authorized third-party business partners.

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact & Identity Details: Full name, professional email address, contact telephone number, job title, and organization name when submitting contact inquiries or RFP requests.
  • Project & Architectural Specifications: Technical requirements, infrastructure parameters, identity provider specifications (such as PingFederate, Okta, CyberArk, or ForgeRock), and business objectives shared during consultation requests.
  • Academy & Internship Applications: Educational background, university affiliation, degree credentials, resume/CV links, GitHub/portfolio links, and code submissions for technical evaluations.
  • Direct Communications: Notes, emails, chat messages, or correspondence exchanged via official email addresses or verified WhatsApp business communication channels.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • Device & Network Identifiers: Internet Protocol (IP) address, operating system, browser type and version, language settings, and device telemetry.
  • Usage Analytics: Pages visited, navigation paths, referring URLs, session durations, click-stream metrics, and interaction timestamps utilized exclusively for infrastructure optimization and threat detection.
Financial Information Excluded We do not collect or store credit card numbers or banking PINs on this public website. All commercial payments and enterprise retainers are executed via authorized corporate banking channels and invoiced through formal procurement agreements.

04

Zero-Trust Security Controls & Data Storage

As an enterprise organization whose core practice is Zero-Trust IAM engineering, we apply the exact same continuous-verification rigor to our internal data management as we do to Fortune 500 client infrastructures:

  • Continuous Cryptographic Protection: All external web communications enforce Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS). Data at rest is encrypted using military-grade Advanced Encryption Standard (AES-256).
  • Least-Privilege & Role-Based Access: Access to internal systems and communication repositories is restricted strictly to vetted personnel on a verified need-to-know basis, protected by hardware-bound Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
  • Automated Posture & Threat Monitoring: Continuous telemetry monitoring, automated intrusion prevention systems, and rigorous code audits across our repositories.
  • Physical & Cloud Resiliency: Data hosting operates within enterprise-certified cloud data centers conforming to ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2 Type II, and PCI-DSS physical security criteria.
05

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Cookies are compact text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you access websites. We maintain a minimal, privacy-centric cookie posture:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for standard website navigation, load balancing, and CSRF protection. These cannot be disabled without impairing core functionality.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Aggregated, non-identifying telemetry to measure platform responsiveness, page loading speeds, and visitor engagement trends.
  • Functional Cookies: Preserves user interface state, such as mobile drawer states or acknowledgment flags.

You can configure your browser preferences to alert you when cookies are placed or to reject all cookies outright. Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain interactive elements of the platform.

Absolute Non-Sale Guarantee DeccanIDentity Software Pvt. Ltd. has never sold, monetized, rented, or traded personal data to third-party data brokers or marketing aggregators, and will never do so under any circumstances.

08

Retention & Sanitization

Personal data is retained exclusively for as long as necessary to satisfy the specific purposes for which it was gathered:

  • Client Project Records: Retained for the active duration of the Master Services Agreement plus statutory audit periods (typically 7 years under Indian Companies and Tax legislation).
  • General Business Inquiries: Retained for a maximum of 24 months from the last active correspondence unless an active commercial relationship ensues.
  • Internship & Recruitment Files: Retained for 12 months following application review for future talent pool consideration, after which resumes are permanently purged.
  • Server Security Logs: Automatically rotated, truncated, and purged on a 90-day rolling cycle.

Upon expiration of statutory retention windows, data is irreversibly wiped using cryptographic erasure procedures compliant with NIST SP 800-88 standards.

09

Your Data Subject Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, including the India DPDP Act 2023 and the GDPR, you enjoy enforceable statutory rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access & Confirmation: The right to obtain confirmation as to whether your personal data is being processed, and to receive a clear copy of stored records.
  • Right to Rectification: The right to correct inaccurate, obsolete, or incomplete personal data held in our systems.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to Be Forgotten”): The right to request the deletion or purging of your personal records where valid legal grounds for retention have expired.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: The right to halt specific processing activities based on legitimate interests or direct communication.
  • Right to Data Portability: The right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is predicated on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without impacting prior lawful processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@deccanidentity.com. We respond to all verified statutory requests within 30 days without charge.
