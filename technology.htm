Digital Trust & Security

Identity Technologies

At DeccanIDentity, we specialize in cutting-edge solutions that redefine the landscape of digital trust, Zero-Trust access control, and identity governance.

Core Pillars

The 5 Pillars of Enterprise Digital Trust

Our technology portfolio addresses the complete lifecycle from automated verification to ongoing governance.

AI-Powered Identity Verification & Orchestration

Move beyond traditional checks with real-time, AI-driven identity proofing and risk scoring.

  • Fast, accurate identity verification powered by machine learning algorithms
  • Seamless biometric integration: facial recognition, voice, and behavioral analytics
  • Dynamic, fraud-resistant verification layer preventing identity theft
  • Unmatched accuracy, reliability, and regulatory compliance standards

Intelligent Access Control & Governance

Granular control over physical and digital enterprise assets with robust policy enforcement.

  • Role-Based (RBAC) & Attribute-Based (ABAC) dynamic policy frameworks
  • Real-time monitoring, privileged escalation, and fine-grained authorization
  • Enterprise directory integrations: CA SiteMinder, CA Directory, OpenDJ
  • Comprehensive tamper-evident audit trails for SOX, GDPR, and ISO standards

Next-Generation Authentication

Frictionless, modern access for workforce and consumers with passwordless architecture.

  • Passwordless login, FIDO2/WebAuthn, and adaptive Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
  • Open standard compliance: SAML 2.0, OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect (OIDC), LDAP
  • Centralized Single Sign-On (SSO) across hybrid cloud & on-premises applications
  • Context-aware risk-based step-up authentication

Enterprise Identity Management & Compliance

Automate the employee lifecycle while maintaining rigorous compliance alignment.

  • Automated onboarding, role-change entitlement updates, and instant deprovisioning
  • Industry leader implementations: SailPoint IdentityIQ, OneLogin, CA IDM
  • Directory synchronization: Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD), OpenDJ, Active Directory
  • Continuous compliance mapping for SOX, HIPAA, and GDPR audits

Enterprise Apps + AI: The Connected Ecosystem

DeccanIDentity seamlessly converges Web, Mobility, and Artificial Intelligence to create a unified identity and access management experience.

  • Unified Web & Mobile IAM: Consistent, secure, and user-friendly experience across all digital channels and devices.
  • Custom AI-Driven Identity: Tailored identity models that integrate legacy systems, satisfy niche compliance requirements, and automate anomaly detection.

AI + Cybersecurity

Integrating predictive machine learning models to detect credential stuffing, anomalous behavior, and suspicious token re-use in real time.

Enterprise Cloud Apps

Robust ERP, CRM, and cloud platforms engineered with microservices, automated CI/CD pipelines, and high-availability databases.

Generative AI Workflows

Enabling enterprises to securely harness LLMs, internal retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and smart automated agents.
